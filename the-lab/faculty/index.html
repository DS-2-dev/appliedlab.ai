For faculty

Represent your college in the Lab.

Representatives review each step of the projects in their area, keep the relationships with partners, and carry what they observe back into their courses.
Join as facultySee the review gates

Why it matters

From the review to the syllabus

Representatives conduct the operation, and they transfer what they observe into their curriculums. That keeps the skills taught in class aligned with what industry needs.

Who can represent

Every college, department, or club

Any college, department, or club at Weber State may have a Representative with the Lab.

Sign in with your address. An Admin confirms it as a Representative account.

Responsibilities

What a Representative does

  1. 1

    Act as the liaison

    Representatives connect partners and members and keep both sides informed.

  2. 2

    Keep partner relationships

    Establish and maintain relationships with partners in your coverage area.

  3. 3

    Review every gate

    Approve or return posts, plans, and submissions from one Approval Hub.

  4. 4

    Keep milestones on time

    Make sure members execute their milestones on schedule.

  5. 5

    Adjust the team

    Remove or replace members on a project when the work calls for it.

The Approval Hub

Every review in one place

  • One list for every gate

    Posts, plans, and submissions waiting on you sit in one list, filtered by gate.

  • Decide on what was sent

    Each review shows a snapshot of exactly what the member sent, even if they keep editing.

  • Approve or return

    Approve moves the work forward. Return sends it back one step with your feedback, and the member is notified.

Representatives can contact a partner directly, especially before a first post goes live.
