How it works

One line from a posted problem to a delivered tool.

A partner posts a problem, and a faculty Representative reviews it before it reaches students. A member claims it, writes a plan, builds with AI, and delivers the result. Each review approves the work or returns it with feedback.

  1. Stop 1 of 8

    Partner posts

    A title and a short description is enough to post. The post starts as a Draft and goes to a Representative.

    Status while here

    DraftPost stageWaiting on the partner
    Who acts
    Partner

    Any organization with a problem can post, and more detail can be added at any time.

    What happens
    The partner writes a title and a short description of the problem, then sends the post for review. A Representative often contacts a first-time partner while the post is still a Draft.
    What each side sees

    MemberMembers see the post once a Representative has approved it.

    PartnerThe post shows as a Draft with one next step, which is to send it for review.

  2. Stop 2 of 8

    Rep reviews the post

    A Representative approves the post to the Notice Board or returns it to the partner with feedback.

    Status while here

    PendingPost stageWaiting on the Rep
    Who acts
    Representative

    The faculty Representative for the post's area reviews it from the Approval Hub.

    What happens
    The Representative reads the post as the partner sent it and may ask the partner a few questions. Then the Representative approves it to the Notice Board or returns it with feedback.
    What comes back

    Approve moves it to LivePost stage

    Return moves it back to DraftPost stage

    The partner is notified either way.

    What each side sees

    MemberThe post reaches the Notice Board when it is approved.

    PartnerThe post shows Pending until the Representative decides. A return opens with the feedback as the next step.

  3. Stop 3 of 8

    Member claims

    Any member can claim a live post, which starts a project. One post can carry several projects.

    Status while here

    LivePost stageWaiting on members
    Who acts
    Member

    Members claim alone or with a team, and they can also start a project of their own.

    What happens
    A member reads the post on the Notice Board and claims it. The claim starts a project under the post, and other members can still claim the same post.
    What each side sees

    MemberThe new project opens with its first step, which is to write the action plan.

    PartnerThe post lists each member who claimed it, with a project for each claim.

  4. Stop 4 of 8

    Member writes the plan

    The member writes an action plan and sets milestones that show they understand the problem.

    Status while here

    ClaimedLab stageWaiting on the member
    Who acts
    Member

    The member who claimed the post writes the plan, with a team when there is one.

    What happens
    The member writes an action plan and sets the milestones the work will be measured against. When the plan is ready, the member sends it to the Representative.
    What each side sees

    MemberThe project shows Claimed, and the plan and milestones are open to edit.

    PartnerThe post shows a member planning the work.

  5. Stop 5 of 8

    Rep reviews the plan

    A Representative approves the plan so the work can begin, or returns it with feedback.

    Status while here

    PendingLab stageWaiting on the Rep
    Who acts
    Representative

    Reps review every gate from one Approval Hub.

    What happens
    The Rep reads the action plan and milestones exactly as the member sent them, even if the member keeps editing. Then the Rep approves the plan or returns it with feedback.
    What comes back

    Approve moves it to ActiveLab stage

    Return moves it back to ClaimedLab stage

    The member is notified either way.

    What each side sees

    MemberThe plan shows Pending until the Rep decides. A return opens with the Rep's feedback as the next step.

    PartnerThe post shows a member planning the work. The approved plan and milestones open once the Rep approves.

  6. Stop 6 of 8

    Member builds

    The member builds with AI and logs progress against milestones while the partner watches.

    Status while here

    ActiveLab stageWaiting on the member
    Who acts
    Member

    Messages and files for the project open when the plan is approved.

    What happens
    The member builds the answer with AI and marks each milestone as it is finished. The Representative keeps the milestones on time, and the final submission opens once every milestone is done.
    What each side sees

    MemberThe next open milestone is always the next step.

    PartnerMilestones and progress notes update as the member works.

  7. Stop 7 of 8

    Rep reviews the work

    The submission carries the work, links to the working result, and a line on who did what.

    Status while here

    SubmittedLab stageWaiting on the Rep
    Who acts
    Representative

    The Representative for the post's area reviews the finished work.

    What happens
    The member submits the work with links to the result and a line on who did what. The Representative reviews that submission, then approves it or returns it with feedback.
    What comes back

    Approve moves it to DeliveredLab stage

    Return moves it back to ActiveLab stage

    The member is notified either way.

    What each side sees

    MemberThe project shows Submitted until the Representative decides.

    PartnerThe partner is notified when the work is approved, and sees it then.

  8. Stop 8 of 8

    Delivered and showcased

    Approved work goes to the partner, and an Admin approves the showcase story before it publishes.

    Status while here

    DeliveredLab stageWaiting on the partner
    Who acts
    Partner and member

    The partner receives the work, and the member presents it.

    What happens
    The approved work reaches the partner, who can request a meeting with the member. The member drafts a showcase story, and an Admin approves it before anything is published.
    What each side sees

    MemberThe project shows Delivered, and the showcase draft opens.

    PartnerThe work appears on the Approved Work page, where a meeting can be requested.

  9. Partner stage, optional. The partner can adopt the project, which starts an agreement with the member and a second plan.

What a return means

A return is feedback on the next draft.

Every review ends in one of two outcomes. Approve moves the work to its next step. Return sends it back one step with written feedback, and the member or partner revises and sends it again.

Each round keeps a snapshot of what was sent and how it was answered, so the history shows how the work improved.
