How a problem becomes a project

The full journey, step by step

Step 1, the partner

A partner posts a problem

It takes a title and a few sentences. A faculty Representative checks it with the partner, and then it goes on the board.

Step 2, the student

A student claims it and writes a plan

Students work alone or with a team. The plan names the milestones, and a Representative approves it before the work begins.

Step 3, the work

They build it, with AI and with each other

Progress shows against the milestones as it happens. Teams talk, share files and keep their own tasks in one place.

Step 4, the result

Reviewed, delivered and shown

A Representative reviews the work before the partner sees it. Finished work can go on the public showcase, with the student's name on it.
