How a problem becomes a project

The full journey, step by step
On the platform

Partner

Businesses, nonprofits, university offices and clubs that bring problems to the Lab.

  • Post problems for students to take on
  • Answer questions as the work goes
  • Receive the work and decide what comes next
For businesses
On the platform

Representative

Faculty who guide students’ work for organizations and review it at each checkpoint.

  • Review posts, plans and finished work
  • Keep projects moving
  • Look after the organizations in their area
For faculty
On the platform

Member

Students from any major who join the Lab.

  • Claim a problem or start their own
  • Plan, build and log progress
  • Publish what they built
