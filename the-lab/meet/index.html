First meeting

What to expect at a first meeting

  1. 01Arrive and find a seatThe room is open to every student and faculty member, and you can come without signing up.
  2. 02Hear the announcementsOfficers open with what changed since the last meeting, including new problems on the Notice Board.
  3. 03Work through the topicEach meeting has one topic, and members work through it together with their own projects open.
  4. 04Talk to a memberMembers stay afterward to answer questions about joining and to show what they are building.
