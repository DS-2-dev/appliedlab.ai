First meeting

What to expect at a first meeting

  1. 1

    Arrive and find a seat

    The room is open to every student and faculty member, and you can come without signing up.

  2. 2

    Hear the announcements

    Officers open with what changed since the last meeting, including new problems on the Notice Board.

  3. 3

    Work through the topic

    Each meeting has one topic, and members work through it together with their own projects open.

  4. 4

    Talk to a member

    Members stay afterward to answer questions about joining and to show what they are building.

Reminders

Get a reminder before each meeting

Leave an email address and the Lab will write to you before each meeting. The list is only for reminders, and you can come to any meeting without being on it.
