For partners

Bring us a problem.

Post a title and a short description. A faculty Representative helps shape the post, a Weber State student claims it and plans the work, and you watch it move until the result is delivered.

Why it matters

What you leave with

Partners who complete a project leave with the confidence to take risks with AI, and with newcomers who can help transform their industry.

Who can partner

Any organization with a problem

  • Businesses

    Companies of any size with a process to improve

  • Nonprofits and charities

    Organizations stretching a small staff

  • Local organizations

    Groups serving the Ogden community

  • University offices

    Campus departments and services

  • University clubs

    Student organizations with a need

Posting

A title and a short description is all it takes.

Add as much detail as you like, now or later. A Representative reads every post before it goes live, and first-time partners usually hear from one directly.

What your post moves through

  1. Draft
  2. Pending
  3. Live

Live means it appears on the Notice Board, where members can claim it. You are notified when it goes live.

What you get back

What comes back to you

  • Plans

    Each member who claims your post writes an action plan and milestones. A Representative approves the plan before work starts.

  • Progress you can watch

    Follow milestones, read progress notes, and see summary statistics for every project under your post.

  • Approved work

    Work reaches your Approved Work page after a Representative approves it. Request a meeting whenever you are ready.

  • Partner stage, optional

    Adopt a project to keep building with the member. It starts an agreement and a plan of your own.

What is protected

Your organization stays in control

  • Admins approve every public story

    Nothing about your project reaches the showcase until an Admin approves it.

  • Sensitive details are redacted

    Showcase stories leave out names, data, and anything else you mark as sensitive.

  • You see work after it is approved

    A Representative reviews each submission before it is delivered to you.

  • Adopting a project starts an agreement

    Members own what they build. The partner stage sets the terms with the member.

  • Your files stay in your Drive

    Files link to your Google Drive folder, and you can add or remove them from either place.

Questions

Before you post

How much detail does a post need?

A title and a short description. A Representative may ask a few questions before the post goes live, especially on a first post.

Who reviews my post?

A faculty Representative reads every post before it goes live. The Representative may contact you with questions, and you are notified when students can see it.

My organization uses Microsoft 365. Can I sign in?

Yes. You can sign in with a code sent to your email address, which works with any email provider.

Can I meet the student?

Yes. You can request a meeting from your Approved Work page, and partners are welcome at Lab meetings.

What happens after the work is delivered?

The work stays on your Approved Work page. You can adopt the project to keep building with the member, which starts an agreement and a plan of your own.
