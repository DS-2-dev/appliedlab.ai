Principles

How we work with AI, and with each other.

Seven principles guide every project in the Lab. They describe how members use AI and what they owe the people they build for.

Purpose
To bring people together, ask hard questions, and elevate everyone involved.
Culture
Be excellent. Be kind. Work hard.

Seven principles, in the order we teach them

1 of 7

People First

Because AI changes execution, we deliberately choose to rely on each other more. We create an environment where members gather to bounce ideas, inspire one another, and build lasting connections to ensure our culture endures through every turnover in membership.

2 of 7

Top-Down Learning

We start with the end in mind and back into the skills needed to build our vision. By focusing on the target outcome first, we acquire the precise technical capabilities and deep understanding required to realize our project and confidently defend our work.

3 of 7

Idea to Execution

We believe AI bridges the skill gap between a creative vision and its reality. We lean heavily into our own creativity, using AI as the tool to build the projects, tools, and businesses we dream of.

4 of 7

Hard Work

AI removes friction in one place and moves it to another. We embrace the heavy lifting required to solve hard problems, refusing to settle for easy AI drafts when true quality requires deep iteration and grit.

5 of 7

Radical Transparency

We are honest with ourselves and our peers, openly disclosing when and how AI is used in our work to foster a lab culture grounded in candor and trust.

6 of 7

Absolute Accountability

We take total personal ownership of our deliverables. While AI is a powerful assistant, we are the final editors and are fully responsible for verifying every output we produce.

7 of 7

Intellectual Honesty

We commit to mastering the underlying logic of our solutions, challenging ourselves to know why our solutions work.

How it appears

One student, all seven principles

In practice, a student walks into the weekly lab meeting to map out an ambitious vision with peers, using the community to turn raw inspiration into a concrete goal. Working top-down, they define the final deliverable upfront and back into the exact skills and understanding required to build it. By leveraging AI to bridge skill gaps from idea to execution, routine friction is removed, freeing them to put in the hard work of auditing claims, stress-testing assumptions, and elevating project quality. Throughout the process, they log milestones with radical transparency and take absolute accountability for their results, ensuring they possess the intellectual honesty needed to defend every decision when presenting their finished deliverable to an industry partner.
