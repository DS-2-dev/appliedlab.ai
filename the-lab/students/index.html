For students

Learn AI by building something an organization will use.

Members claim problems that partners post, write a plan, and build the answer with AI. A faculty Representative reviews each step, and finished work goes to the partner and the public showcase.

Why it matters

What you leave with

Members leave with the ability to tackle large, ambiguous problems with confidence and to think critically about the world around them. They also leave with the candor and consideration to guide others to do the same.

Who can join

Any major. Any college.

Any student from any college or department at Weber State can join. Members bring the field they study, and AI helps with the parts they have yet to learn.

  • Business and Economics
  • Engineering, Applied Science and Technology
  • Health Professions
  • Science
  • Social and Behavioral Sciences
  • Arts and Humanities
  • Education

Sign in with your @mail.weber.edu address and your account opens as a Member.

What you do

Four things, every semester

  1. 1

    Attend meetings

    Meetings are where members map out ideas with peers and hear what partners need.

  2. 2

    Claim a problem

    The Notice Board lists every live post. Any member can claim any post or start a project of their own.

  3. 3

    Write the plan

    An action plan and milestones show that you understand the problem. A Representative reviews it.

  4. 4

    Build and deliver

    You build with AI, log progress, and submit the work with links to what you built.

Member levels

Three levels, one badge

Your level shows next to your name everywhere it appears, and on your profile. An Admin sets it each semester based on attendance and project work.

AffiliateEntry

Affiliates attend events and get to know the Lab before taking on a project.

  • Project involvement starts at the next level
SponsoredMid-level

Sponsored members keep a Claude account funded by the Lab while they hold the attendance bar.

  • 60% attendance each semester
  • Funded Claude account
SeniorFull involvement

Senior members are assigned to a project and show progress every week.

  • Assigned to a project
  • Weekly progress
  • 60% attendance each semester

Learning outcomes

What you learn

Members learn how AI applies in their field and how to deploy it effectively. By the end of a project you can explain three things.

  1. 1How AI applies in your field, and how to deploy it well
  2. 2Where AI fits in your work and where people are integral to the process
  3. 3How to explain those positions and defend them to a partner

Questions

Before you join

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Members come from every college, and AI covers much of the building. You need to understand the problem well enough to write a plan and defend it.

Does it cost anything?

Meetings are free for students and faculty. Members with an active project get a Claude account funded by the Lab.

Can I bring my own idea?

Yes. Any member can start a project of their own as well as claim a post from the Notice Board.

What happens if my plan comes back?

A returned plan comes with written feedback from the Representative. You revise it and send it again, and each round keeps a snapshot of what was sent.

Who owns what I build?

You do. Members own what they build, and a partner who wants to use it makes an agreement with the member.
